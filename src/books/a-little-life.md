One thing after another for this guy. Almost too sad to comprehend. Definitely see the grief/trauma porn criticisms. Lots of characters but never felt lost or incoherent. Made me miss New York in parts.

I was interested to learn that the author is the editor of T Magazine. I'd like to read more of her work, but you never hear about her other books really.
