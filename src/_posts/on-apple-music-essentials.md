I love the Apple Music Essentials playlists. Great way to get into a band you're just discovering and a great way to get to the singalong classics of your favourite artists.

However…

I've noticed a trend whereby Apple will place a newly released single at the top of the Essentials playlist. I get why, and for some artists maybe it makes sense and maybe the song will become part of their list of big hits. Too often though, it feels like this just breaks the contract of trust I have with them.

If we look at Crowded House for example. If you go searching for a playlist of their "essentials" which were all released in the 90s, I don't think you're expecting to see the tune that came out in 2024 at the top.

Just another small part of the reason I'm going back to an offline music library I can manage myself. Even if it means putting together my own essentials playlists.
